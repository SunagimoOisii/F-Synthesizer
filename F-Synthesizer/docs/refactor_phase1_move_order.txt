REFACTOR Phase 1 Move Order (Baseline)

1) Add wrapper entry points
- Keep current files in place.
- Introduce thin wrappers in target layers without changing behavior.

2) Switch call sites
- Route main/config/midi/io/gui callers to new wrapper APIs.
